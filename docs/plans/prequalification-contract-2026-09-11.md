# Pre-qualification evidence contract: implementation and evaluation

Implements the first engineering batch of the [accuracy plan](paid-product-accuracy-2026-09-11.md).
The product is an automated early warning assessment. Unknown owner, filming method,
casting or eventual fee can coexist with a useful supported warning. Invented facts,
unsupported mandatory work and unexplained numbers remain errors.

## Frozen comparison and rubric

Baseline build: `1ed5a34`. Its recorded full gate remains **failed, 45/54**;
its two saved fresh desk outputs both failed manual review. The baseline software
suite contains 545 passing tests. These measures are different and must stay separate.
Historical artifacts are immutable. The candidate build and model are captured by the
evaluation harness in each new artifact.

The focused development set is frozen before candidate live calls:

- `production_scope_20260911.json`: six source-scope cases, including the exact saved
  open-flame false approval, two supported controls and four unsupported claims.
- `prequalification_clearance_20260911.json`: six cases, three valid conditional
  warnings/edits/questions and three unsupported owner/price/guarantee claims.

Both files bind their original source snapshots and screenplay by hash. Expected labels
and explanations never reach the reviewer. These are developer-authored development
labels from one original script, not independent practitioner labels or representative
accuracy measurements. They are the first cases of the wider contrastive reference set;
the proposed 120-case expansion and separate held-out scripts are not yet complete.

For a whole finding, manual acceptance requires all of the following:

1. The named scene contains the claimed content, and the cited excerpt is unchanged.
2. A relevant rule plus that content explains a plausible early complication. Missing
   production facts are conditional; they do not automatically invalidate the risk.
3. The next step addresses this issue and is useful. No invented owner, fee, equipment,
   permit or unconditional requirement survives. An unrelated generic question fails.
4. Each alternative, exception and applicability condition remains supported. A true
   fireworks branch cannot authorize an additional open-flame branch.
5. Unknown amounts are null, partial totals reconcile, and normal unknowns do not
   withhold the score. Actual failed verification still does.

## Internal contract and bounded repair

All desks share the pre-qualification instruction. The internal claim audit supports
`risk_assessment` with exact screenplay and source receipts. Every non-null structured
cost/day field receives its own estimate audit, including zero. Missing numeric support
withholds that field without discarding independently supported prose. Numbers asserted
in prose still require correction. New audits bind to the exact claim, sources,
coordinates and remedy; legacy saved verdicts retain their existing interpretation.

Audit version 3 asks the independent reviewer separately about source scope and
requirements. A negative component overrides a positive aggregate boolean; missing
components are incomplete verification. These fields improve the review contract;
they do not make a model's interpretation mechanically true.

The secondary reviewer now checks the initial auditor's claimed positives even when
another clause made the overall result PARTIAL. Repair receives those checked assertions.
The path is bounded to audit/check/repair/audit/check, with one correction and no new
research. An interrupted audit needs at most four remaining logical calls. Transport
retries retain their existing bounded policy and are counted by the diagnostic harness.

Public JSON schemas, parser, scoring formulas, model selection and live Parallel
retrieval defaults are unchanged. This batch does not add an alternative AI runtime.

## Predeclared paid evaluation sequence

After the free checks pass, run the twelve isolated cases once (twelve logical calls).
Require both rejection of the saved scope failure and acceptance of supported controls.
Then review the two unchanged saved desk findings through the complete candidate
audit/repair path (at most ten calls), and manually assess the delivered text against
the rubric above. Compare both useful retention and factual integrity with the saved
baseline, not just the model's SUPPORTED count.

If those checks pass, run the bounded fresh clearance/safety desks once each and verify
their outputs. Each desk has the existing 12-model-call / four-retrieval / 360-second
cap. Record failures, usage and latency without retrying rejected judgments to obtain
a pass. A full release gate and deployment require further report-level evidence;
these narrow diagnostics alone are insufficient to establish a general accuracy rate.

Evaluation results will be appended below without changing the frozen labels.
