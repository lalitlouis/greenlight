## A. Accuracy — paths by which a false or unsupported finding renders

- **A1 HIGH [record] Overturn guards flip sourcing rejections to SUPPORTED.**
  `verification.py:617-655` (stated-fact) and `:469-495` (absence); `_SOURCING_GROUND_RE`
  at :394-398. When the rejection sentence carries no numbers, :639 falls back to EVERY
  capitalized word in the reason and :649 (`if nums and …`) skips the number-beside-name
  check, so two names co-occurring in <=3 scenes flip the verdict. The absence path accepts
  any absence phrase within 90 chars of a quote that exists. Evidence: current gate record
  **F1004** renders SUPPORTED — the rejection was `citation_offtopic` (a prop-design blog);
  the overturn text says the script states "Daily, Gloucester, Times", the newspaper's own
  name, never disputed. `case_reservoir_dogs` F2004: a correct script_misstatement (Zippo vs
  match) flipped. `case_little_miss_sunshine` F1005: "GROUND PARTIALLY OVERTURNED: the
  'unstated fact' objection was false" written onto a rejection that raised no such
  objection. Original reasons truncated to 200 chars (:437, :455) — the true ground is
  unrecoverable from the record. Fix: require `nums`; drop the whole-reason proper-noun
  fallback; never full-flip `premise_unsupported`/`citation_offtopic` (strike the ground at
  most); keep the full original reason; add the three record cases as regression tests.
- **A2 HIGH [probe] Language census regexes miscount.** `prepass.py:93-107`.
  `r"chink s?"`/`r"kike s?"` carry a literal space: "A chink in the armor" → slur `'chink '`
  (phantom TC-CENSUS-SLURS item); "chinks", "He's a kike." → not counted. `\bfuck\w*`,
  `\bshit\w*` miss motherfucker/bullshit/dipshit; `nigger\w*` misses "nigga". The work-item
  note says "exact — do not recount" while `find_in_script("fuck")` (toolbelt.py:446-471, no
  `\b`) DOES count compounds — census and desk tool disagree on the same script. Fix:
  `chinks?`, `kikes?`, `(?:mother)?fuck\w*`, `(?:bull|dip|horse|chicken)?shit\w*`,
  `nigg(?:er|a)\w*`; pin with tests.
- **A3 HIGH [record] Safety prompt cites the wrong CSATF bulletin.** `safety_underwriter.py:128-130`
