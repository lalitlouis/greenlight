How your screenplay is protected

An unreleased script is the most sensitive document in this industry. Here is what protects yours, control by control — and, just as plainly, what we have not certified yet. Last updated August 28, 2026 · ScriptRisk is in beta.

Bucket access alone yields ciphertext: the seal is applied before your script reaches storage, and the key never lives beside the data.

Encryption

  • In transit: TLS everywhere — browser to us, and between every internal service and provider.
  • At rest, twice: your script and its analysis are encrypted at the application layer (AES-128-CBC with HMAC authentication, via Fernet) before they reach storage, on top of Google Cloud Storage's own at-rest encryption. Access to the storage bucket alone yields ciphertext.
  • Key handling: the encryption key lives in Google Secret Manager, never in code or configuration files, and is rotated on credential-exposure events and major releases (last rotation: August 2026).

Access

  • Sign-in: Google OAuth only — we never see or store a password. Sessions expire.
  • Ownership is structural: a signed-in analysis can only be read or deleted through the owner's session; the storage layout makes reaching another user's run impossible by construction, not by policy.
  • Anonymous runs are protected by unguessable identifiers — the link is the capability, and it is yours to share or not.
  • Operations: production access is limited to a single administrator; hardening of every underlying provider account (hardware-backed two-factor, access reviews) is part of our TPN preparation below.

Your work is never training data

  • We do not train models on your screenplay, and our AI processing runs under enterprise terms (Google Cloud Vertex AI) that do not permit training on your content.
  • Production logs are engineered to carry no screenplay text — titles, findings, and excerpts are kept out of operational logging by design, and this promise is enforced in code.

Deletion that actually deletes

  • Delete any analysis at any time — from My reports signed in, or via the report page for anonymous runs.
  • Deletion removes the script, the analysis record, generated PDFs, and ownership markers together — not just the listing.

Monitoring and response

  • Failed analyses page the operator by email within minutes — a paid run failing silently is treated as an incident, not a statistic.
  • Every deploy is gated: the full test suite, lint, and a compliance scan must pass, and deploys are refused while any customer analysis is running.
  • Infrastructure runs on Google Cloud, inheriting Google's own SOC 2, ISO 27001, and related attestations for the physical and platform layers.

What we have not certified — yet

Badges only mean something if you know what they prove, so here is our honest status:

  • TPN (Trusted Partner Network) — the MPA's content-security program, built for vendors who handle unreleased material. Membership and the Blue Shield self-assessment are next on our roadmap after our current release cycle.
  • SOC 2 — an independent audit of security controls over time. We operate the underlying practices described on this page; the formal audit is planned as we grow into studio and enterprise work.

We will not display a badge we have not earned. When these change, this page changes.
