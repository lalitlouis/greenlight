The department

Four desks, one producer's answer

A studio doesn't ask one person "is this script risky?" — it runs four independent departments that never talk to each other, then reconciles them. ScriptRisk mirrors that, desk for desk.

Clearance Counsel

Rights & clearances — the desk that keeps you out of court.

  • Music: a played recording needs TWO licenses — sync for the composition, master for the recording — from different owners. The desk chases both chains.
  • Brands: a logo that's simply visible in a scene is usually fine; a brand your characters mock, misuse, or blame for harm is a lawsuit risk (disparagement). The desk tells the two apart and only flags the second.
  • Real people: right of publicity, and for negative depictions the defamation / false-light line — what's in the documented record vs. what the script invented.
  • Artwork, tattoos, film clips: anything depicted on camera that someone else created — including the 95-year public-domain arithmetic.
  • Venues & locations: permit vs. location agreement vs. "that casino will never say yes — plan a set."

Files: sync_license, master_use_license, right_of_publicity, defamation_false_light, trademark_use, trade_libel_venue, artwork_license…

Ratings Board

What will the MPA actually rate this — and what would move it?

  • Counts the drivers on the page: language (every F-bomb, cited by scene), violence, drug use, sexuality, thematic elements.
  • Predicts the rating from evidence, not opinion: your script's content profile against 6,302 released films — "6 of your 8 nearest comparables are rated R or stricter," each with a source — plus the measured CARA boundary with a 90%-per-rating-group coverage guarantee (92% aggregate; thin classes pooled — see methodology).
  • Feeds the What-If simulator: check cuts, and the comparables re-run live.

Files: rating_language, rating_violence, rating_drug_use… plus the rating prediction with its comparables.

Safety Underwriter

The line producer's early-warning system for physical risk.

  • Stunts, pyro, water work, vehicles, firearms, animals, minors near any of the above — with the insurer and CSATF-bulletin rules cited.
  • Remedies read like a shooting plan: which specialist to hire (stunt coordinator, armorer, animal handler), what it costs, what it adds to the schedule.
  • Comedic gags get the real method — rig, dummy, VFX takeover — not a prohibition.

Files: stunt_*, fire_safety, minor_safety, animal_safety, firearms_blanks… with est. costs and added days.

Territory Censor

What gets cut or banned, market by market — US · UK · CN · UAE.

  • Researches each territory's actual rules with geo-targeted search — the CN questions are asked from China's web, the UAE's from theirs.
  • Distinguishes depicted from merely recounted (a ghost story told in dialogue is not a ghost on screen), and story-critical content from an easy localized cut.
  • A mandatory closing checklist sweeps six sensitive axes per strict territory so nothing slips: supernatural, drugs, alcohol, religion, sexuality, state authority.

Files: territory_cn_*, territory_uae_*, territory_uk_*, territory_us_* — each citing the regulator's practice.

Why you can trust it

Accuracy is enforced by code, not requested by prompt

1

No citation, no finding

The filing tool structurally rejects any flag without a citation. A finding the model "just knows" cannot exist in your report.

2

Quotes must be real

Every cited excerpt is checked character-for-character against what was actually retrieved this run. Paraphrase or invent, and the filing bounces.

3

Blinded cross-examination

An independent verifier re-reads each citation without the desk's reasoning. Rejected findings never touch the score — and they're shown, struck through, with the reason.

4

Numbers come from data

The rating prediction is a nearest-neighbor search over released films, not a model's opinion — and each comparable carries its own source.

5

Honest unknowns

What can't be verified becomes an open question, not a confident guess. "We couldn't prove it" appears in your report exactly that way.
