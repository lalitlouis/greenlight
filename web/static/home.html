What ScriptRisk isn't

Not a clearance-house report

E&O insurers require a report from a recognized clearance house; ScriptRisk doesn't discharge that requirement. It gets you to that conversation earlier, with fewer surprises.

Not legal advice

Findings are research with sources, built to hand to your attorney — not a substitute for one. Cost figures are industry rules of thumb, not quotes.

Not a completeness guarantee

Every finding shown is cited and independently verified — but the desks scan what's on the page in four territories and the categories listed in How it works. Absence of a flag is not clearance.

Find the expensive problems while a rewrite is still free

Rewriting around a needle-drop at page 40 costs nothing. Discovering it in post can cost $80,000. ScriptRisk surfaces the money problems at script stage — cited, verified, and anchored to your pages.
