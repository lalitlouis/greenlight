When to use The Black List

When the writing is ready and you want human scores and industry eyeballs — its marketplace reaches agents and producers, which no analysis tool replaces. Use ScriptRisk first: walk in knowing your clearance exposure before anyone asks.

When to use Filmustage

When you're greenlit and scheduling the shoot — its breakdown-to-schedule-to-budget flow is the deepest of these tools. It budgets what's on the page; ScriptRisk researches what the page will trigger in the outside world: licenses, ratings, censors, insurers. They're neighbors, not substitutes.

When to use Prescene or ScriptReader.ai

When you want development notes — Prescene for studio-grade story intelligence, ScriptReader.ai for cheap scene-by-scene feedback. Both trust a model's judgment; neither cites a source or checks its own claims. That's the line ScriptRisk exists on: every finding cited, every citation independently verified, rejections shown.
